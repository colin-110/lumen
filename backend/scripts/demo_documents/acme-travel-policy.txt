ACME CORP - TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance Operations.

1. FLIGHTS
Employees book economy class for any flight under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights between 6 and 10 hours. Business class requires written approval from a Vice President and is only allowed for flights longer than 10 hours. Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance unless the trip is urgent; late bookings need a one-line justification in the expense report.

2. HOTELS
The nightly hotel cap is 180 USD in most cities. In New York, London, Tokyo and Zurich the cap is 260 USD per night. Taxes are included in the cap. Stays above the cap need manager approval before booking.

3. MEALS
The daily meal allowance is 75 USD domestically and 95 USD internationally. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client dinners. Client entertainment above 150 USD per person requires the client's company name and attendee list.

4. GROUND TRANSPORT
Use public transport or ride-share where practical. Rental cars are allowed when cheaper than the alternatives; book the compact class. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at 0.67 USD per mile.

5. SUBMITTING EXPENSES
Submit expense reports within 30 days of the trip ending. Attach an itemised receipt for every expense above 25 USD. Reports submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed. Corporate card statements are reconciled on the 5th of each month.

6. QUESTIONS
Contact travel@acme.example for bookings and expenses@acme.example for reimbursement questions.
