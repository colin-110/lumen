MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between Acme Corp ("Client") and Northwind Analytics Ltd ("Provider")
Agreement number: MSA-2026-017. Signed 20 February 2026.

1. SERVICES
Provider will host, maintain and support the Client's data warehouse, including daily backups, monitoring, and up to 20 hours per month of analyst support.

2. TERM
The agreement starts on 1 March 2026 and runs for 12 months, ending 28 February 2027. It renews for further 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days' written notice.

3. FEES
The monthly service fee is 4,200 USD, fixed for the initial 12-month term. Additional analyst support beyond 20 hours is billed at 140 USD per hour, with prior written approval from the Client. No other charges apply unless agreed in writing.

4. INVOICING AND PAYMENT
Provider invoices monthly in advance on the 1st of each month. Payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.0% per month.

5. SERVICE LEVELS
Provider guarantees 99.9% monthly availability. For each full 0.1% below the target, the Client receives a 5% credit on that month's fee, up to a maximum of 25%.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days' written notice if the breach is not fixed in that period.

7. CONTACTS
Client: procurement@acme.example. Provider: billing@northwind.example.
